psycopg2 is licensed under the GNU LGPL v3 (LGPL-3.0.txt) and is linked
dynamically. It bundles libpq and its dependencies; the upstream texts for the
ones not already shipped elsewhere are in this folder (Kerberos, OpenLDAP, Cyrus
SASL, libselinux). On Linux it also bundles libxcrypt and keyutils, which are
under the GNU LGPL v2.1 (LGPL-2.1.txt).
